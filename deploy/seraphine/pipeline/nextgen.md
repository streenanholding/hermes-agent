## The play
Run both tracks at once, and use the free one to earn credibility for the paid one.

Register on Patient Access today — no form, no fee, no approval queue. It gets us a working NextGen connection and something demonstrable within days rather than months. Then submit the Distributor form separately for the provider-scoped data and write-back we actually need.

When the Distributor conversation happens, the fact that we already have a working Patient Access integration changes the tone completely — we arrive as an existing developer on their platform asking to expand scope, not as a stranger asking for access. The data-scope fees are unpublished and therefore negotiable, and negotiating from inside is always better.
