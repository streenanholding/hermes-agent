## The play
Deprioritise, but keep one lever in reserve.

This is the only vendor confirming both upfront AND ongoing monthly developer costs while publishing neither number, behind an NDA we must sign before seeing documentation. For a startup managing cash, that is a poor use of early effort. Pursue it when a paying client running AdvancedMD asks us to, and let their demand justify the spend.

THE LEVER, WHEN WE DO ENGAGE: request their §170.404 certified-API transparency disclosure BY NAME. Every ONC-certified vendor is legally required to publish one, and it must disclose what they may and may not charge for certified API access. Asking for it by regulation number is the single most effective way to get a straight answer from an opaque vendor, and it surfaces any no-cost FHIR endpoint that is not marketed on their commercial pages.
