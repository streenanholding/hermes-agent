## The play
Free API, free platform, free marketplace listing, in writing. Nobody else on this list offers all three. This should be our first medical integration.

THE SANDBOX GAP IS THE REAL CONSTRAINT, AND IT IS SOLVABLE WITH A RELATIONSHIP, NOT MONEY. No sandbox means we develop against a live practice. So line up a cooperative design-partner practice BEFORE registering, not after. One friendly Greenway office that will let us test against their environment turns this from the hardest technical setup into the easiest commercial one. Ask the sales team whether any prospect in the pipeline runs Greenway.

THE FREE GALLERY LISTING IS AN UNDERRATED MARKETING ASSET. Most vendors charge for marketplace placement or gate it behind a live customer. Greenway does neither. A listing there is free distribution to their entire client base — get it as soon as the integration works.
