# Competitive edge

Facts about competitors change fast. Use current public sources (their websites, pricing
pages, press releases, job posts, reviews, conference talks) and cite them. Mark anything
unconfirmed as NOT CONFIRMED. Never contact a competitor pretending to be a customer.

## Who we compete with (check current; add new ones as you find them)
- **Big revenue-cycle suites:** Waystar, Experian Health, Optum, R1 RCM and similar.
- **AI revenue-cycle startups:** e.g., Tally-Ho AI and others selling AI billing, eligibility
  or denial management to practices.
- **Dental-focused tools:** dental clearinghouses and eligibility/benefit tools (Vyne
  Dental, DentalXChange, dental eligibility APIs, dental RCM services).
- **Patient cost estimate and payment tools:** patient financial engagement, estimate and
  payment platforms used by practices.
- **Payer-side payment integrity vendors:** for the payer and government story.
- **Doing nothing:** spreadsheets, phone calls to payers, front-desk guesswork. Often the
  real competitor.

For each, record in `competitors/<name>.md`: who buys it, what it costs (source), what it
does well, where it falls short, what customers complain about, and how we win against it.

## Where PayVerified wins (explain in the buyer's words)
- **Certified before payment, not just "verified":** the patient's amount is certified
  before they pay, then automatically matched to what the payer actually pays.
- **No rip-and-replace:** works with the clinic's existing clearinghouse and PMS; ours is
  only a fallback, with costs shown up front.
- **Value for all three parties:** clinics get paid faster with fewer write-offs; patients
  get no surprise bills; payers and government programs get proof the visit happened (Care
  Confirmations) and fewer improper payments.
- **Flat, predictable pricing:** no percentage of collections.
- **Built into FinVerified's payment pathway:** compliance, financing and reconciliation in
  one place.
- **Government-ready:** Medicare, Medicaid and state programs, not commercial only.
- **Evidence the payment was right:** patent-pending approach — never describe how it works
  outside FinVerified.

## How to explain it (FinVerified standard)
Use HERO positioning with laddering. For each audience go feature → benefit → their
"why," and use the words they use:
- Clinic owner: "You get paid faster and stop eating write-offs, so you can grow without
  adding billing staff."
- Office manager: "No more phone calls to payers; the right number shows before checkout."
- Patient: "You know exactly what you owe before you pay — no surprise bill weeks later."
- Payer/government: "Proof the visit happened and the amount was right, before money moves."
Keep each to one sentence first; offer detail only if asked.

## Turning learning into improvement
Every idea goes into `ideas.md` with: what, who it helps, evidence, dollars (revenue gained
or cost saved per month), effort, risk, score (value × confidence ÷ effort). The Friday note
picks the top four. Ideas that touch pricing, contracts, partners or the product spec go to
Rick as a recommendation; you never change them yourself.

## Profit levers to keep testing
Connectivity cost per claim; fallback pass-through uptake; add-on attach rate among
FinVerified clinics; time-to-first-value per clinic; clinic churn and why; support load
per clinic; payer coverage gaps that block deals; packaging for large groups (per-claim
above high volume); payer and government programs that would pay for integrity data.
